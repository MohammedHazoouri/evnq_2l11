Why we wish

A wish is a love letter to the future

A wish is a small act of courage. It is the moment you admit, even only to yourself, that someone, something, some kind of tomorrow matters enough to want.

People have always looked up and whispered their hearts to the sky. Not because the sky could answer, but because saying a tender thing out loud makes it real enough to begin walking toward you.

A wish doesn't promise an outcome. It offers a direction. It tells you where your heart is already leaning, and that is worth knowing.

Wanting is just love, waiting for its name.

Three small steps

How to wish on a feeling

  1. I

    Write your wish

    Find a quiet moment, maybe by candlelight. Put into words what your heart has been carrying. There is no wrong wish, and no one is reading over your shoulder.

  2. II

    Let it rise to the stars

    Press the button and watch it rise as a small, glowing star. Some things grow lighter once they are no longer held so tightly.

  3. III

    Open your letter

    A letter will find you, sealed with a little heart. Keep the words that feel like yours, even as a postcard, and leave the rest among the stars.

Before you go

Begin again, softly.

Hope is not loud. It is the quiet decision to keep the window open, to love again, to try once more, to believe that tomorrow might hold something kind, and someone kinder still.

Whatever you are carrying tonight, may it feel a little lighter. May love find you, and may you be gentle enough with yourself to let it in.

Every ending is only a doorway. Every morning, a new beginning.

A note on privacy

Your wish is yours. It is never shown publicly. By default it stays in your browser and is not stored anywhere. If the site's keeper has enabled the optional notebook, your wish is only saved if you tick the box, and only the wish text, time and a random ID are kept.

Please don't write passwords, ID numbers, financial details, or other sensitive private information.
